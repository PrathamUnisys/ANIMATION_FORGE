# Executive Producer User Manual

## Navigating the 13 Studio Modules
1. **Dashboard**: View active production stage, pending human approvals, recent assets, cost estimates, and click **CONTINUE PRODUCTION** to jump directly to the next required gate.
2. **Projects**: Create, duplicate, archive, restore, export, or import multi-episode projects.
3. **Creative Studio & Production Pipeline**: Develop stories through the 13 screenplay passes and inspect scene/shot breakdowns.
4. **Production R&D Lab & Toolchain Manager**: Review candidate tools across 11 dimensions, authorize Proofs of Concept, and lock `APPROVED_TOOLCHAIN.json`.
5. **Review Studio & Quality Control**: Inspect technical QC reports (`APPROVED_TECHNICALLY`) and exercise final human creative judgment (`Approve`, `Request Revision`, `Reject`).
