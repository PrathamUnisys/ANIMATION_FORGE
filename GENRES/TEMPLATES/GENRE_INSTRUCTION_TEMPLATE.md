# Genre Instruction Template

## Genre identity and promise

- **Genre ID:** `GENRE_<NAME>`
- **Display name:** [Name]
- **Genre family:** [Primary family]
- **Audience promise:** [What experience this genre should deliver]
- **Typical audience expectations:** [Expectations to satisfy, challenge or deliberately subvert]

## Narrative engine

Describe the recurring source of story movement: desire, conflict, stakes, opposition, escalation and resolution.

## Story structure and pacing

Describe useful structures, act/sequence expectations, escalation patterns and pacing principles. Avoid fixed formulas unless the genre genuinely requires them.

## Characters and relationships

Describe useful character roles, archetypes, motivations, relationship dynamics and ways to avoid flat or stereotyped characters.

## World, setting and rules

Describe setting conventions, internal logic, continuity constraints and the role of world-building where relevant.

## Dialogue and voice

Describe dialogue conventions, subtext, vocabulary, exposition control and genre-specific voice considerations.

## Visual and sound opportunities

Describe recurring visual language, spectacle, atmosphere, sound and music opportunities. These are creative directions, not proof that a tool can generate them.

## Common failure modes

List clichés, structural weaknesses, tonal mistakes and confusing conventions to avoid.

## Production implications

Identify likely scene, asset, environment, animation, audio and effects demands. Do not prescribe a tool unless the project requirements and tool catalogue support it.

## Screenplay review checklist

- [ ] Genre promise is clear.
- [ ] Audience expectations are deliberately handled.
- [ ] Conflict and stakes support the selected genre.
- [ ] Character behavior is credible within the genre.
- [ ] Pacing and tone are consistent.
- [ ] Genre conventions feel intentional rather than copied.
- [ ] Secondary genres complement the primary genre.
- [ ] Production demands are identifiable and feasible.

## Context interaction

State how this genre combines with other genres and which conventions must remain intact. State explicitly that the channel style guide controls presentation and channel identity, not genre logic or story canon.
