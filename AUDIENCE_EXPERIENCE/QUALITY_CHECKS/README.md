# Audience Experience Quality Checks

This directory is reserved for reviewed quality checks. Checks should evaluate a screenplay or finished video without claiming to predict audience behaviour with certainty.

Potential review dimensions:

- Audience promise and premise clarity.
- Opening comprehension and interest.
- Character motivation and emotional investment.
- Narrative progression and information clarity.
- Suspense, clues and payoff.
- Visual storytelling and animation readability.
- Audio opportunities and intelligibility.
- Ending satisfaction and series continuity.
- Title/thumbnail/opening consistency.
- Production feasibility.

When checks are created, distinguish:
1. Mandatory correctness or policy gates.
2. Creative quality assessments.
3. Evidence-backed recommendations.
4. Optional techniques.
5. Experimental hypotheses.

No numeric weighting, pass threshold or retention target is defined in this scaffold. Those should follow the research and be tested against actual work.
