# Creative Quality Rubric

**Status:** DRAFT PROPOSAL. Advisory review framework; no numeric gate is active.

## How to use

Review the screenplay itself before reading companion metadata. Select dimensions relevant to genre, format, audience promise, and production scale. Do not demand that every story maximise every dimension.

For each finding, cite an exact scene, beat, line, or passage. Separate observable evidence, interpretation, likely impact, and recommendation. Mark unchecked areas NOT_TESTED.

## Review dimensions

| Dimension | Review questions |
|---|---|
| Audience promise | Is the intended experience clear, and does the actual story fulfil it? |
| Premise and story engine | Is the central situation specific, understandable, and capable of sustaining the format? |
| Protagonist agency | Does the protagonist pursue an objective and make consequential choices? |
| Character motivation | Can the audience understand what major characters want and why they act? |
| Stakes and opposition | Is there a meaningful cost, obstacle, or opposing force? |
| Causality and coherence | Do major events and solutions follow from established facts, choices, and rules? |
| Setup and payoff | Are important objects, relationships, promises, and rules developed and fulfilled? |
| Clue and reveal integrity | Are clues available in time, deductions traceable, and red herrings explainable? |
| Scene progression | Does each scene change information, conflict, emotion, relationship, danger, or visual understanding? |
| Dialogue and subtext | Is dialogue speakable, character-specific, purposeful, and not duplicating the image? |
| Visual storytelling | Do images, staging, and performance communicate story information clearly? |
| Audio storytelling | Are voice, silence, ambience, music, and effects meaningful and intelligible? |
| Emotional credibility | Are emotional turns earned through setup, choice, and consequence? |
| Genre integrity | Does the story respect its selected genre without becoming formulaic? |
| Originality and distinction | Does the story have a specific identity beyond familiar conventions? |
| Ending and payoff | Does the ending answer or intentionally frame the central question and fulfil setup? |
| Production feasibility | Are required scenes, assets, animation, and audio practical or explicitly flagged? |
| Runtime integrity | Are estimates transparent and appropriate to the format, and are measurements labelled accurately? |

## Severity

- **BLOCKER:** Fundamental contradiction, missing prerequisite, or integrity defect prevents meaningful review or downstream use.
- **MAJOR:** Substantial story, comprehension, motivation, causal, emotional, or production problem.
- **MINOR:** Localized weakness with limited impact.
- **NOTE:** Optional opportunity, preference, or uncertainty.

Severity must be justified by evidence and consequence. Do not inflate subjective taste into a blocker.

## Finding format

Record finding ID and severity; dimension; exact scene/beat/passage; observable evidence; interpretation and likely audience/continuity/production impact; recommended action or alternatives; confidence and review limitation; and whether addressed in the next revision.

## Revision response

For each MAJOR or BLOCKER finding, respond with:
- ADDRESSED — cite changed revision and evidence.
- PARTIALLY_ADDRESSED — explain what remains.
- NOT_CHANGED — explain creative reason or trade-off.
- DEFERRED — state dependency or decision required.

Re-review affected downstream scenes and payoffs. A revision is not automatically better because it is longer or contains more metadata.

## Scores and decisions

Scores are optional descriptive aids only. Do not calculate or use an overall average as a pass/fail gate. Do not compare reviewers/models as calibrated. Critical findings stand independently of scores.

A creative review may recommend revision, indicate readiness for human review, defer, or report insufficient evidence. It cannot approve the screenplay or change approval state. Technical QC and human approval remain separate.
