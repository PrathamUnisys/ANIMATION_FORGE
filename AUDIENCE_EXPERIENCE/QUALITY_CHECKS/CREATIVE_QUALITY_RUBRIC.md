# Creative Quality Rubric

**Status:** DRAFT PROPOSAL. Advisory review framework; no numeric gate is active.

## Review dimensions

Review only dimensions relevant to the selected genre, format, and story. For each finding, cite an exact scene, beat, line, or passage and separate observable evidence from interpretation.

| Dimension | Review questions |
|---|---|
| Audience promise | Is the intended experience clear, coherent, and fulfilled? |
| Hook and opening | Does the opening create a relevant reason to continue and connect to the story? |
| Premise and story engine | Is the central situation understandable and capable of sustaining the episode? |
| Causality and coherence | Do events and solutions follow from established facts, rules, and choices? |
| Character agency | Are objectives, motivations, opposition, and decisions legible? |
| Clue and reveal integrity | Are important clues available in time and does the reveal account for them? |
| Scene progression | Does each scene change information, conflict, emotion, relationship, or visual understanding? |
| Dialogue and subtext | Is dialogue speakable, character-specific, and purposeful? |
| Visual storytelling | Do images and performance carry story information clearly? |
| Emotional credibility | Are emotional turns earned by setup and consequence? |
| Genre integrity | Does the story respect the selected genre's promises and rules? |
| Originality and distinction | Does the story have a specific identity beyond familiar conventions? |
| Ending and payoff | Does the ending answer or intentionally frame the central question and fulfil setup? |
| Production feasibility | Are the required scenes, assets, animation, and audio practical or explicitly flagged? |
| Runtime integrity | Are estimates transparent and appropriate to the intended format? |

## Severity

Use the established severity vocabulary:
- BLOCKER: A fundamental contradiction, missing prerequisite, or integrity defect prevents meaningful review or downstream use.
- MAJOR: A substantial story or audience-comprehension problem that should normally be addressed before human approval.
- MINOR: A localized weakness with limited impact.
- NOTE: An optional opportunity, preference, or uncertainty.

Severity must be justified by evidence and consequence. Do not inflate subjective taste into a blocker.

## Findings format

For every finding record: finding ID; severity; dimension/category; exact scene/beat/passage reference; observable evidence; interpretation and likely audience/production impact; recommended action or alternatives; and confidence/review limitation where relevant.

## Scores

The research report suggests a 0–10 scorecard and an example overall gate. Existing repository schemas also contain optional score fields. Neither creates an approved scoring policy.

Until a separate calibration exercise and human approval:
- Scores are optional descriptive aids only.
- Do not calculate or use an overall average as a pass/fail gate.
- Do not compare scores across reviewers/models as if they were calibrated measurements.
- Critical findings must be reported independently of any score.
- Do not let a score replace evidence or human judgement.

## Review decisions

A creative review may recommend revision, indicate readiness for human review, defer, or report insufficient evidence. It cannot approve the screenplay or change its approval state. Technical QC and human approval remain separate.
