# YouTube Analytics Interpretation Playbook
**Status:** DRAFT. This is not an automated diagnosis engine.

## Metrics to record when available
- Impressions and click-through rate.
- Views and watch time.
- Average view duration and average percentage viewed.
- Intro retention and relevant retention segments.
- Retention spikes and dips.
- Subscriber change attributed to the video, where available.
- Returning viewers and traffic sources.
- Shorts viewed versus swiped away, where applicable.

Record metric definition, collection date, reporting window, video format and source. Mark unavailable metrics as unavailable; never estimate them.

## Diagnostic prompts
| Observation | Investigate | Possible next action |
|---|---|---|
| Low CTR | Topic/package fit, title and thumbnail clarity, traffic context | Test another package |
| Early drop | Promise fulfilment, opening clarity, audience mix, sound/visual legibility | Review opening and traffic source |
| Mid-video dip | Information dump, confusing clue, repeated beat, production issue | Inspect matching scene |
| Spike | Rewatch, memorable moment, confusion or other behaviour | Inspect segment before concluding |
| Strong viewing but few returns | Series identity, ending and next-episode promise | Review continuity and audience response |

These are prompts, not causal diagnoses.

## Interpretation safeguards
- One video is not enough to establish a channel-wide rule.
- Compare similar format, runtime, topic and traffic context.
- Consider audience mix, promotion, upload context and seasonality.
- A dip identifies a segment to inspect; it does not prove a scene is bad.
- A spike can be positive, negative or ambiguous.
- Do not infer private retention or watch time from public view counts.

## Experiment loop
State one hypothesis, isolate a variable where practical, choose comparable videos or a supported platform test, define primary/secondary/guardrail metrics, set an observation window with a sample rationale, report uncertainty, and update a playbook only after human review.
