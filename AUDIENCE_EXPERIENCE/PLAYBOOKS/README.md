# Audience Experience Playbooks

This directory is reserved for reviewed, versioned guidance derived from the audience-experience research.

Planned playbooks:

- Hook and opening design.
- Retention and story pacing.
- Mystery, suspense and horror storytelling.
- Visual storytelling for animation.
- Audio storytelling.
- Audience engagement and community.
- Titles, thumbnails and packaging.
- Shorts and long-form adaptation.
- Analytics interpretation.

No playbook is active until its research findings have been reviewed, scoped, versioned and approved. Do not place universal timing rules, retention targets or mandatory script formulas here before evidence review.
