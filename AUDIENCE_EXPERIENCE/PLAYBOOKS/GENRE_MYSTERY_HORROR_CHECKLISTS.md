# Mystery and Horror Integrity Checklists
**Status:** DRAFT. Use only when the selected genre profile makes the checklist relevant.

## Occult Detective / Supernatural Mystery
- [ ] The case matters to the protagonist and has a clear investigative objective.
- [ ] The central mystery can be stated as a question.
- [ ] Important clues appear before the reveal.
- [ ] Each deduction can be traced to evidence already shown.
- [ ] Red herrings are explainable and do not depend on unfairly hidden information.
- [ ] Supernatural rules and limitations are introduced before the solution relies on them.
- [ ] The investigator's solution is not a deus ex machina.
- [ ] The ending answers the central question or deliberately frames what remains unresolved.
- [ ] Hook and packaging accurately reflect the case.

## Dark Folklore
- [ ] The cultural or folkloric source is identified and researched.
- [ ] Local names, practices and context are not carelessly blended across traditions.
- [ ] Documented tradition is distinguished from adaptation choices.
- [ ] Variants are acknowledged where relevant.
- [ ] Moral or lesson arises from the story rather than forced dialogue.
- [ ] Cultural, historical and rights questions are routed for human review.

## Psychological Horror
- [ ] Fear connects to character psychology, relationships or perception.
- [ ] The audience can follow what the character believes and why.
- [ ] Unreliable narration is signalled fairly when used.
- [ ] Fear escalates through meaningful changes, not unrelated shocks.
- [ ] The ending is unsettling by design, not confusing because key information is missing.
- [ ] Character choices remain understandable even when perception is distorted.

## Cosmic Horror
- [ ] The phenomenon suggests scale or rules beyond ordinary human understanding.
- [ ] The story does not over-explain the entity merely to close every ambiguity.
- [ ] Clues imply a larger framework without contradicting local facts.
- [ ] Character responses and consequences follow from what they experience.
- [ ] Resolution preserves the intended sense of scale or insignificance.
- [ ] Visual and audio choices support unease without replacing story logic.

Mark each item PASS, ISSUE, NOT_TESTED or NOT_APPLICABLE and cite a scene or evidence. These are review prompts, not a universal weighted score. A human reviewer decides whether an issue blocks progression.
