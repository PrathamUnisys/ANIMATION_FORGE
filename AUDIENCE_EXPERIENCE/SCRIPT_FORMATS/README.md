# Audience-Facing Script Formats

This directory is reserved for production-ready screenplay templates after the YouTube storytelling and script-format research is complete.

Planned templates:

- Animated long-form episode.
- Animated Short.
- Shared-universe episode.
- Occult detective / supernatural mystery episode.
- Folklore adaptation.
- Historical mystery.

The existing screenplay conventions and schema remain authoritative until a reviewed change is approved. Do not introduce a competing active screenplay format from this preparatory directory.

Each future template should distinguish:
- Spoken dialogue and narration.
- Visual action and staging.
- Scene and shot references.
- Sound and music cues.
- Story beats and continuity references.
- Optional audience-experience annotations.
- Production requirements and revision status.

Retention annotations should support writers and reviewers; they must not force arbitrary cliffhangers or interrupt a coherent story.
