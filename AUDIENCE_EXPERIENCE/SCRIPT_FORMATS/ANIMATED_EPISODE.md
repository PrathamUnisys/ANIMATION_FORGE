## Outline
1. **Cold open / hook (starting guide 0:00–0:30):** opening visual, first line/sound, viewer question and later fulfilment beat.
2. **Setup (0:30–1:30):** protagonist, setting, case and stakes.
3. **Investigation / development (1:30–4:00):** progressive clues, choices, red herrings and new information.
4. **Midpoint development (around 4:00 in the report's example):** reversal or evidence that changes interpretation.
5. **Climax:** confrontation or decision; bring clues and rules together.
6. **Resolution:** answer the central question, show emotional consequence and optionally add a restrained series connection.

These timing windows are illustrative heuristics, not fixed scene durations.
