## Character pressure
For each major character, capture want, fear/cost, strategy, blind spot/need where relevant, pressure, choice, and consequence. Do not force a conventional arc where the story does not call for one.

## Story spine and outline
Map the causal chain from disruption through choices, obstacles, escalation, climax, and consequence. Optional prompts:
1. Opening/hook: image, action, sound, question, and later fulfilment.
2. Setup: protagonist, world, objective, conflict, and stakes.
3. Development: attempts, obstacles, evidence, decisions, and consequences.
4. Turn/reinterpretation: new information or consequence that changes the situation, if useful.
5. Climax: consequential action or choice.
6. Resolution: answer, emotional consequence, and optional series thread.

For each major turn, state what causes it and why the next action follows.
