# Audience Experience Deep Research — Synthesis
- Source: Library report **Executive Summary(1)**.
- Status: DRAFT_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW.
- Purpose: turn research into templates and draft guidance before governed runtime integration.

## Recommendations preserved
1. Genre-tailored templates for 6–10 minute episodes and 30–60 second Shorts.
2. A 30+ item hook library using Mystery, Visual Anomaly, Emotional, Supernatural and Suspense categories.
3. A Retention Map recording viewer knowledge, questions, new information, emotional tone and pacing/visual focus.
4. A playbook for curiosity, information order, beat pacing, teasers, variety and payoffs.
5. Four genre checklists: Occult Detective, Dark Folklore, Psychological Horror and Cosmic Horror.
6. Visual storytelling guidance for composition, shot types, camera, lighting, motifs and animation effort.
7. Audio guidance for voice, effects, music, silence, mixing and narration.
8. Title/thumbnail packaging aligned with the story promise.
9. Analytics interpretation for impressions, CTR, intro retention, average duration, spikes, dips, subscriber growth and returning viewers.
10. A creative-review rubric for hook, premise, structure, clue logic, character, visuals, audio, engagement and production feasibility.

## Timing proposals from the report
The report proposes an approximately 0–30 second cold open, setup around 0:30–1:30, investigation around 1:30–4:00, midpoint development around 4:00, hook attention within about 15 seconds, many beats around 15–45 seconds, teasers near one-third/two-thirds, and meaningful progress roughly every minute. A 45–60 second Short is shown with an immediate hook, core and payoff/tag.

These values are retained as initial editorial heuristics, not platform mandates or universal empirical laws. Adapt them to runtime, comprehension, story complexity and production constraints. Do not insert artificial cliffhangers to satisfy a timer.

## Metric and scoring caveats
A low CTR does not prove the thumbnail is at fault. An early drop does not prove the hook failed. A spike may mean rewatching, confusion or a memorable moment. A dip is an inspection point, not a cause. Compare similar formats and multiple videos.

The report proposes 0–10 creative scores and an example overall gate around 7, with critical failures overriding averages. This branch records the dimensions but activates no numeric gate. Calibration and human review are required before a threshold can block progression.

## Evidence limitations
The source report uses internal citation labels (for example [3] and [24]) for YouTube help material and practitioner guidance. This synthesis preserves the report's claims but does not independently resolve every reference. Before activating any rule, resolve the exact sources, record publication/access dates and context, seek contradictory evidence and test Animation Forge-specific hypotheses using actual pilot work and channel analytics.
