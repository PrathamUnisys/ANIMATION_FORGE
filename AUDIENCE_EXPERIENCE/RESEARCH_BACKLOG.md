## Evidence grades
- **STRONG:** Multiple high-quality sources or robust primary evidence in a relevant context.
- **MODERATE:** Credible evidence, but limited context, replication or applicability.
- **LIMITED:** Small, indirect, mixed or methodologically constrained evidence.
- **PRACTITIONER_CONVENTION:** Common professional technique without strong direct empirical validation.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Plausible proposal to test on Animation Forge output.
- **CONTRADICTED_OR_UNRESOLVED:** Credible disagreement or insufficient evidence.

## Required research behaviour
Record publication/access dates, exact sources, format/genre/audience context, sample and methodology where available. Seek contradictory evidence. Separate platform policy from storytelling practice. Do not infer private analytics from public view counts. Do not promote one creator's success to a universal causal rule.

## Adoption decisions
For each finding decide whether to accept generally, accept only for a format/genre, keep optional, test as an experiment, reject or defer. Accepted findings require versioned playbooks, human review and explicit tests. Do not modify agents directly from raw research.
