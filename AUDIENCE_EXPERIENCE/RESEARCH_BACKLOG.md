## Evidence grading

Use these labels consistently:

- **STRONG:** Multiple high-quality sources or robust primary evidence support the claim in a relevant context.
- **MODERATE:** Credible evidence supports the claim, but context, replication or applicability is limited.
- **LIMITED:** Small, indirect, mixed or methodologically constrained evidence.
- **PRACTITIONER_CONVENTION:** A commonly used professional technique without strong direct empirical validation.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** A plausible proposal to test on Animation Forge's own output.
- **CONTRADICTED_OR_UNRESOLVED:** Credible sources disagree or available evidence is insufficient.

Evidence grade is about support for a claim, not a guarantee that a technique will work for every video.

## Source priority

1. Official YouTube documentation and announcements for platform definitions and current features.
2. Peer-reviewed studies, systematic reviews and academic publications for scientific claims.
3. Reputable books, universities and established practitioners for craft methods.
4. Creator case studies with transparent dates and methods.
5. Independent analytics/industry sources with disclosed methodology.
6. Creator communities and Reddit for qualitative experience, clearly labelled as anecdotal.

## Required research behaviour

- Record publication date and access date.
- Prefer primary sources and link to the exact source.
- Capture the studied format, audience, genre, platform and sample when available.
- Do not extrapolate public view counts into private watch-time or revenue metrics.
- Do not convert one creator's success into a universal causal rule.
- Seek contradictory evidence before recommending a default.
- Separate platform policies from storytelling practice.
- State where animation-specific evidence is absent.
- Do not invent universal retention targets or timing rules.
- Treat initial 6–10 minute and approximately 45-second targets as project assumptions to test, not conclusions.

## Research integration decisions

For every workstream, the reviewer should decide:

- Accept as a general principle.
- Accept only for a specific format or genre.
- Keep as an optional technique.
- Convert into an experiment.
- Reject as unsupported or unsuitable.
- Defer pending more evidence.

Accepted findings should be converted into versioned playbooks and explicit tests. Do not modify production agents directly from raw research notes.
