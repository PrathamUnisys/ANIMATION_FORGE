# SCRIPT_LAB_10 — Controlled Screenwriting Benchmark Plan

**Status:** PLANNED. No benchmark revision or human approval is implied by this plan.

## Objective

Evaluate whether the proposed Screenwriting Constitution and Audience Experience guidance improve screenplay clarity, causal integrity, audience promise, and production usefulness without flattening genre or voice.

## Benchmark episodes

| Episode | Primary evaluation |
|---|---|
| EP10 — The Ministry of Small Apologies | Satire, dialogue, escalation, character motivation, and payoff |
| EP07 — The Echo Census | Mystery logic, clue availability, information sequencing, and complicity/motivation |
| EP04 — The Cartographer of Small Years | Emotional setup, pacing, visual metaphor, and restrained payoff |

## Controlled procedure

1. Resolve and record the exact source screenplay revision and file hash.
2. Preserve the original as read-only benchmark input.
3. Record the exact brief, Story Bible/canon context, genre profile, format, and constraints used.
4. Capture baseline creative review and all available deterministic QC evidence. Mark unavailable checks explicitly.
5. Create an Audience Plan proposal: intended audience, audience promise, format, central question, and known assumptions.
6. Generate alternative opening hooks and explain each hook's question, story connection, and payoff location.
7. Review title/thumbnail concepts against the same promise; do not optimize packaging independently of story truth.
8. Build or revise the outline and scene cards only where evidence supports a change.
9. Produce a new screenplay revision without overwriting the original.
10. Generate a Retention Map whose scene and beat references resolve against that exact revision.
11. Run the available deterministic validator and record the exact checks actually executed.
12. Run an independent creative critique with evidence, severity, interpretation, recommendation, and unchecked areas.
13. Compare original and revised versions by dimension; record improvements, regressions, and unchanged areas.
14. Submit the exact candidate revision for human review. Do not mark it approved or production-ready without the configured approval path.

## Comparison criteria

- Audience promise and opening fulfilment.
- Causal coherence and character motivation.
- Clue/reveal fairness where relevant.
- Scene purpose and information progression.
- Dialogue and genre voice.
- Visual and audio storytelling opportunities.
- Emotional credibility and ending payoff.
- Production feasibility and runtime estimate quality.
- Requirement compliance, with story beats/clues distinguished from exact text.

## Controls

- Keep the original screenplay and its review immutable.
- Do not change the episode brief or approved canon silently to make a revision pass.
- Record provider/model/method provenance; label manual or mock review honestly.
- Do not compare numerical scores unless a calibrated scoring policy has been approved.
- Do not treat YouTube retention heuristics as pass/fail criteria.
- Do not infer that a benchmark revision is superior from metadata completeness alone.

## Acceptance evidence

A benchmark is complete only when it contains exact source and candidate revision identifiers/hashes; context and instruction provenance; original and candidate scripts; scene-linked Retention Map; deterministic QC results by individual check; independent creative critique; comparative findings with evidence; and human decision or explicit pending-review status.

## Out of scope

No YouTube analytics integration, automated publication, database migration, schema activation, or production breakdown is part of this benchmark plan.
