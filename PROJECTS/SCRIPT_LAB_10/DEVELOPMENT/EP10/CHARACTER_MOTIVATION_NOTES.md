# EP10 Character Motivation Notes

**Scope:** Improved revision of EP10 (*The Ministry of Small Apologies*), grounded in baseline + brief. No canon invented.

## Noor (protagonist) — junior clerk, sincere, weary
- **Want (scene objective):** Close Mr. B’s file sincerely without falsifying harm or feeding an absurd escalation.
- **Super-objective:** Do work that means something in a place built to process feeling into metrics — stay human without breaking.
- **Why it matters now:** The **the unread complaints shelf** holds real, unattended hurts; spending institutional effort on **the grievance that never happened** is a moral and practical insult to those waiting. Noor’s sincerity is not naïveté but a quiet ethic.
- **Stakes (personal):** Becoming another stamp; complicity in a system that notices people only when injured. Professional risk if she mishandles escalation, but the deeper cost is ethical erosion.
- **Key choices & causes:**
  - SC01: Tries to help within rules (selects **the apology form 7B**) — cause: duty + habit of care.
  - SC02: Proceeds despite mismatch — cause: pressure of queue/process and Mr. B’s insistence; glance at shelf creates friction.
  - SC03: Alarm at ministry-wide escalation — cause: understands scale of absurd harm (procedural) vs real harms ignored.
  - SC04: Asks “What do you actually want?” — cause: metrics failed; empathy is the only remaining tool. Writes margin apology — cause: can only speak truth she can stand behind.
  - SC05: Reframes as habit/precedent — cause: seeks a filing path that does not lie. Creates HABITS tray and takes a real file — cause: restored purpose.
- **Emotional progression:** Weary competence → strained compliance → principled alarm → clear empathy → quiet resolve.
- **Arc:** Subtle but present (agency/clarity, not personality overhaul): from “close the file” to “change how I file what matters.”

## Mr. B (aggrieved citizen) — aggrieved by nothing, persistent
- **Want:** Receive an official apology; be processed, named, seen.
- **Underlying need:** Recognition without having to be broken first. “Quiet, being fine” = invisibility.
- **Why it matters:** In a culture of forms, a stamp is proof of existence; he has no qualifying injury, so he manufactures a claimable absence.
- **Key choices & causes:**
  - SC01–02: Escalates petty/absurd demands — cause: only language the Ministry hears is grievance; entitlement masks need.
  - SC03: Leans on escalation — cause: wants outcome, still armoured.
  - SC04: Admits wanting to be seen — cause: Noor addresses him as a person, not a field; safety to be vulnerable.
  - SC05: Accepts unscored truth — cause: finally addressed without being asked “what was broken.”
- **Credibility note:** Behaviour is comic but psychologically legible; a small tell (ritual neatness, waiting to be called) foreshadows reveal without spoiling it.

## Auditor Vell (sincerity auditor) — crisp, metric-bound
- **Want:** Maintain measurable integrity of **the sincerity audit**; prevent unscored/false filings and procedural chaos.
- **Motive:** Institutional order; belief that unmeasured remorse is unverifiable (and therefore abusable).
- **Opposition function:** Not a villain — embodies the system’s logic; pressure is procedural (escalation rules) rather than personal malice.
- **Credibility of discretionary filing (improved):** Vell refuses to register margin as scored remorse (**the audit's sincerity score** remains inapplicable) but can log an **unscorable precedent/habit notation** under Form 7B’s miscellaneous conduct provision when (a) no citizen injury is claimed, (b) the statement names institutional conduct, and (c) escalation risk is documented. This is a narrow administrative discretion consistent with a bureaucracy that still has edge-case clauses — reluctant, recorded, not “heart-warming” surrender. Preserves satire (system can only absorb truth as an exception) while making action causal.

## Senior Clerk (minor, O.S./ambient)
- **Function:** Establishes stamping rhythm and normalized absurdity; no arc required.

## Relationship dynamics
- Noor↔Mr. B: adversarial transaction → brief human recognition → dignified closure.
- Noor↔Vell: subordinate vs gatekeeper → negotiated procedural truth.
- Noor↔Shelf (motif): conscience; relationship shifts from avoidance to action.
