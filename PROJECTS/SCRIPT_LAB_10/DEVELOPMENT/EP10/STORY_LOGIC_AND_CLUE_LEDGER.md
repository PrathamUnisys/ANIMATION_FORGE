Vell cites Procedure 4 escalation (ministry-wide auto-apology for systemic non-apology) and revision deadline. *Cause:* metric rule + escalation mechanism stated.

5. **SC05:** Vell rejects margin as scored remorse (dial still inapplicable) but Noor cites Form 7B Section 7B-Precedent (unscorable conduct notation when no citizen injury claimed and escalation risk recorded). Vell exercises narrow discretion, logs “RECEIVED — NOT SCORED.” Noor files in HABITS tray and returns to real shelf file. *Cause:* explicit procedural basis + recorded escalation risk + no claimed injury = credible filing path; preserves Vell’s metric integrity.

**No unresolved contradictions found.** Escalation, scoring, and exception are each rule-grounded in-world.
