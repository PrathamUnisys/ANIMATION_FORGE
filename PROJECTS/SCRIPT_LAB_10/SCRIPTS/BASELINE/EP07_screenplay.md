FADE IN:

SC01
**EXT. TOWN SQUARE COUNTING - DAY**

*A bright, orderly town square. A holographic census board hovers above a kiosk: clean numbers, clean lines. DEVI (30s, census officer, tablet in hand) walks with MAYOR LENS, scanning faces in a morning queue.*

DEVI  
Headcount is simple. Stand, be seen, be counted.

*She taps her tablet. A soft chime per person. The board ticks: 408… 409… 410… 411… 412.*

DEVI (CONT’D)  
That’s everyone in the square. **The 412 heads** match the morning roll.

MAYOR LENS  
Then we’re done early. The state likes early.

DEVI  
I verify by address. Doors don’t forget.

*Devi turns her tablet to “door mode.” A map of the town blooms — each dwelling pulsing with a registered number.*

CUT TO:

SC02
**EXT. TOWN STREETS / DOORWAYS - DAY**

*Montage: Devi walks street by street, scanning **the door numbers** — 1 through 413 glowing in sequence on mail slots, lintels, gateposts. Residents wave, confirm names. The tally climbs.*

DEVI (V.O.)  
One door, one household record. Twelve years I’ve trusted that.

*She stops at a narrow lane. Door 413: a small, kept house with a swept step and a light on inside. The tablet pings: REGISTERED. OCCUPANT: [FIELD BLANK].*

DEVI  
Registered… but no name.

*She knocks. No answer. A neighbour peeks out from 412.*

NEIGHBOUR  
No one lives there, officer. Not anymore. Not ever, really.

DEVI  
Your door says 412. Hers says 413. Doors don’t argue.

*Devi looks back at the square count in her mind: **the 412 heads** — but **the count of 413 by doors**. One extra. Her jaw sets.*

CUT TO:

SC03
**INT. CENSUS OFFICE - DAY**

*Screens, printers, a ticking wall clock counting down to **the midnight census lock**. Mayor Lens paces while Devi overlays datasets.*

MAYOR LENS  
It’s a glitch. A phantom address. Clear it and lock us at 412.

DEVI  
Phantoms don’t sweep their steps. Someone lives at 413 — the town has just agreed not to see them.

MAYOR LENS  
This is **the town that forgot a resident** by necessity, Devi. After the clinic closed, records were… simplified. The state reassigned staff. We moved on.

DEVI  
You moved a person out of a sentence. Not out of a house.

*Devi pulls archived audio: a voice log tagged DOOR 413 — a woman saying “I’m home” on a hundred different evenings. The waveform is human, present, uncounted.*

DEVI (CONT’D)  
If I lock 412, the system auto-assigns 413 a number without a name. She becomes a state fiction. My own family was miscounted once — I know what an error erases.

*Clock ticks louder. Lens softens, fear mixing with shame.*

MAYOR LENS  
If you’re right, naming her embarrasses us.

DEVI  
If I’m wrong, the door will tell me. I’m going back.

CUT TO:

SC04
**EXT. DOOR 413 / TOWN STREETS - NIGHT (BEFORE MIDNIGHT)**

*Blue evening. Rain threatens. Devi returns to door 413 with a portable lamp. She knocks again — patient, human.*

DEVI  
I know you’re here. I’ve counted **the 412 heads**, and I’ve counted **the door numbers**. You are the difference. I’m not here to move you. I’m here to say you count.

*A long pause. The lock turns. NURSE AMARA (40s, tired, dignified) opens the door, holding a kettle as if caught mid-routine.*

AMARA  
They told you no one lived here.

DEVI  
The town told me. Your door didn’t. What’s your name?

AMARA *(after a breath)*  
Amara. I was the clinic nurse. When it closed, they struck me from the roster to balance a sheet. Easier to forget a nurse than to admit a failure.

*Devi’s tablet glows: one unfilled field waiting. Thunder murmurs.*

DEVI  
Amara, I have until **the midnight census lock**. I can write you in as a person, not a placeholder.

AMARA  
And the town?

DEVI  
Will have to remember out loud. I’ll stand with you when they do.

*Amara nods — a small, brave permission.*

CUT TO:

SC05
**INT. CENSUS OFFICE - NIGHT (MIDNIGHT)**

*The countdown hits seconds. Mayor Lens watches, conflicted. Devi sits at the terminal, Amara beside her, visible on the verification feed for the first time.*

SYSTEM (V.O.)  
Auto-assignment pending for unverified unit 413.

DEVI  
Override. Manual entry.

*She types: AMARA — DOOR 413 — RESIDENT. Not a number, a name. She adds a human gloss in the margin: “Present. Omitted by record, not by life.”*

*The clock strikes twelve: **the midnight census lock** engages. The board resolves — 413, named.*

MAYOR LENS *(quietly)*  
The town will have to answer for this.

DEVI  
Good. Answers are how you stop being **the town that forgot a resident**.

*Amara exhales, tears held back, counted at last. Devi closes the tablet — not triumphant, just relieved.*

AMARA  
Thank you for knocking twice.

DEVI  
Thank you for opening.

*Outside, lights come on in window 413, warm against the night. The count holds — true, human, complete.*

FADE OUT.
