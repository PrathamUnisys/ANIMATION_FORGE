# THE SALTED LIGHTHOUSE LOG - CORRECTED v2

A 10-minute memory thriller. Intercut present-time lamp repair with log-time memory. Visual storytelling over exposition.

**ESTIMATED RUNTIME:** Approximately 600 seconds / 10 minutes. SC01 110 sec + SC02 130 sec + SC03 130 sec + SC04 120 sec + SC05 110 sec = 600 sec.

**COMMITTED GENRE RULES - OPENING SIGNAL:** J. is not a supernatural presence and no ghost appears on screen. All paranormal beats are misreading and dissociation from concussion: distant boat lights read as a man calling, and a second hand in the log is Mara's own dissociated handwriting. No later beat contradicts these rules.

**CHARACTERS:** MARA QUINN (24, lighthouse keeper trainee; fragmented then steady). J. - a handwriting in the log only, never a performer, never spoken dialogue. RADIO VOICE (V.O.) - base control, for time checks.

**NOTE ON VISUAL LANGUAGE:** Present time is handheld, cold and dark. Log time is macro ink-scratch close-up that intercuts and then fuses. Oil lamp flare is the only warm light source. A clinic discharge card in Mara's coat in SC01 films the genre signal in-story: concussion - memory gaps - misreading - split handwriting possible.

**PRODUCTION NOTE - SLUG CLARIFICATION:** SC01 uses a single heading INT. LIGHTHOUSE BURNING ROOM - NIGHT. No second descriptive slug is placed inside the burning room. The clarification that the action is inside, not an exterior establishing shot, lives in this note only.

FADE IN:

SC01
INT. LIGHTHOUSE BURNING ROOM - NIGHT

VISUAL: Darkness, circular burning room, black glass, cold lamp brass in close-up. Sea below the rock barely visible.
AUDIO: Sea washing below the rock, wind against glass, dead foghorn silence. No foghorn.

Oil lamp FLARE SPUTTERS and DIES.

SMASH TO BLACK. A GASP. A choked cough.

MARA QUINN's eyes snap open in darkness, ON THE FLOOR, cheek against cold stone.

CLOSE-UP: Salt crusted on her skin and hair. White smudges on stone from her fingers.

Handheld camera lurches with her as she pushes up, head spinning, collapsing back.

MARA
Where-

MARA (CONT'D)
What day is it?

She crawls to the great lamp. CLOSE-UP: Cold dark glass. She turns the valve. Nothing. She strikes a match. Flare blooms briefly over the lamp, then DIES.

MARA (CONT'D)
I didn't tend you.

She drags herself to a basin, splashes seawater over her face and washes salt from her skin.

MARA (CONT'D)
Keeper trainee... Mara Quinn... I should-

She finds a clinic discharge card in her coat pocket and holds it to the match flare.

CLOSE-UP - CARD: CONCUSSION - MEMORY GAPS - MISREADING - SPLIT HANDWRITING POSSIBLE.

MARA (CONT'D)
(reading)
Split handwriting...

She staggers to the radio, turns the knob. Only static.

MARA
Hello? Burning room to base? Hello?

She turns to the wall CALENDAR, counts forward with moving lips, hand freezing.

MARA (CONT'D)
Three days.

She confirms for the room and for herself the calendar note that radio confirms the relief boat in three days.

MARA (CONT'D)
Relief boat in three days.

She looks at the dark lamp, then her empty hands.

MARA (CONT'D)
Fourteen days... I lost fourteen days.

MARA (CONT'D)
What did I do?

CUT TO:

SC02
INT. LIGHTHOUSE BURNING ROOM - NIGHT

CONTINUOUS.

VISUAL: Present-time lamp repair at the workbench, flare light, split-screen.
AUDIO: Metal CLANKS, ink scratch fusing present and log time.

Mara attacks the lamp with a wrench. Flare sputters and fails. Her elbow knocks a shelf. A heavy log book slides out and falls open to warped pages.

Mara lifts the salt-stained log on the bench with both hands as if it weighs too much.

MARA
My log.

Macro detail: Pages warped by seawater, salt crystals glittering on the paper, ink bled at edges. Her handwriting is blocky and hurried.

MARA (CONT'D)
(reading)
Day one. Flare weak. Wind southwest. Mara on watch...

AUDIO: Ink scratch begins, sharp and close.

PRESENT TIME: Mara cleaning the wick.
LOG TIME: The same words appear on the page in real time.

PRESENT TIME: Mara tightening a nut.
LOG TIME: Ink scratches another line. Edits accelerate. Two times fuse.

MARA (CONT'D) (reading)
Saw lights... counted boats... wind rising...

She turns a page. Her entry ends mid-line. Beside it she studies the second hand in ink labelled J. and her breath catches.

SPLIT-SCREEN begins.

LEFT: Mara's hand reading in present time.
RIGHT: Macro of a second hand WRITING beside hers. Never shown wider than the hand.

Mara covers the page with her palm.

MARA
No. That's not...

She lifts her hand. Split-screen holds.

SUPER (in ink): J. On watch with M.

MARA (CONT'D)
J?

She flips pages. More J. entries correcting her dates and steadying lamp logs. J.'s writing is tighter and firm. Hers is increasingly shaky.

CLOSE-UP: Mara's left-handed smudge dragged across fresh ink under flare light. She touches the smudge with her fingertip.

MARA (CONT'D)
Who are you?

She checks the wall for a photograph - no second face. A second bunk - no. A second lamp - no. She looks back at the page. The second hand is still writing in the log-time half.

MARA (CONT'D)
There's no other keeper on the roster.

MARA (CONT'D) (whisper)
Who wrote the second hand in the log?

DISSOLVE TO:

SC03
EXT. LIGHTHOUSE ROCK - DAWN

VISUAL: First grey light, wet black rock in flat sea, doorway to burning room.
AUDIO: Seabird cries over the rock, foghorn off, wind, pencil tally marks.

Mara enters booted, carrying the log, moving slowly and dizzy. She crosses the rock in wind.

She stops at the open doorway, crouches at the threshold for a radio-time check, listening to static break.

MARA (into radio)
Burning room, time check?

RADIO VOICE (V.O.)
Base time 05:17. Relief boat still three days out. Check again at 18:00.

Mara marks the time in the log margin and crosses one calendar box in her head. To herself she counts the tally she makes beside the entry, and she recounts the boat count of nine against her memory of fewer boats.

MARA
One... two... I remember... fewer.

CLOSE-UP: Log margin. Nine tally marks in old ink. Fresh pencil count beside it. TALLY: 9

MARA (CONT'D)
Nine boats. I counted nine.

MARA (CONT'D)
I didn't see nine. I... no.

She kneels at the doorway looking inside. She remembers the lamp kept dark while she wrote the entry she now re-reads.

SUPER - LOG ENTRY: Phantom boat. Man in water. Called from below. J. says light him. M. refused.

MARA (reading, shaking)
Kept the lamp dark to protect the rock light...

She looks through the doorway at the dry stone floor inside. She kneels beside the wet footprints inside the dry burning room, fresh prints dissolving on dry stone.

CLOSE-UP: Barefoot prints leading from basin to lamp. Narrower where the salt water edge has dried.

Mara pulls off her own boot, places her bare foot beside the print.

VISUAL: Her foot and the print are the same length. Heel drag matches.

MARA
Mine?

She flips to the log entry she wrote beside the fact she now checks.

SUPER - LOG ENTRY: Boots off - salt in seams. Walked to lamp barefoot. Floor wet.

Mara understands the prints are Mara-consistent: she walked barefoot to tend the lamp during the blackout, leaving prints in salt water that are now drying. The door was locked from inside. No second keeper.

MARA (CONT'D)
I walked in here barefoot. I wrote that. I wrote all of it.

She looks at the boat count, then the prints. Her distress is guilt, not proof of an intruder.

MARA (CONT'D)
I let a boat die... I kept it dark while someone drowned?

INTERCUT: Dawn light on her face. Log entry on page. Her face and the entry match.

MARA (CONT'D)
There was a visitor... I refused him.

CUT TO:

SC04
INT. LIGHTHOUSE BURNING ROOM - NIGHT

Night later. She has worked through the day and has not rested. Flare light sputters weakly. Fog presses the glass.

VISUAL: Workbench, log open to both hands, low-angle flare light raking across paper to show grooves.
AUDIO: Ink scratch, radio static, distant sea.

Mara works at the table by flare light. She lifts her page to the flare to test pressure and injury, not the earlier clue.

She does a second countdown beat: calendar and radio-time check. She turns the calendar page and circles the new count, then keys the radio.

MARA (into radio)
Burning room, time check?

RADIO VOICE (V.O.)
Base time 21:04. Relief boat 48 hours out. Two days.

Mara stares at the calendar page - relief boat circled in two days. The deadline has tightened since dawn.

MARA (CONT'D)
Two days. One day gone.

She returns to the log. CLOSE-UP: Under raking flare light, deep pen-pressure grooves indent the paper in both Mara's lines and J.'s lines. Same depth pattern. Same stroke catch at the same word.

She holds her thumb to the light. A fresh cut, still healing, catches.

SUPER - LOG ENTRY (both hands, same hour): Cut thumb. Grip shifted.

She flexes her thumb. Pain shifts her grip left and tilts the pen away from the cut.

CLOSE-UP: In both Mara's and J.'s lines at that same hour, the pen tilts in the identical direction, away from the same thumb cut, at the same pressure. One person wrote both lines at the same time with the same injured thumb - not a copy made later.

SPLIT-SCREEN merges.

LEFT: J.'s hand writing across the page.
RIGHT: Mara's hand reading.

The halves push together. One hand completes the other's sentence.

Mara stops breathing.

CLOSE-UP: Her hand finishes the line J. started. Same pressure. Same thumb tilt from the same injury. Same hand, same moment.

MARA
Oh. It's mine.

MARA (CONT'D)
J. is my dissociated hand. From the concussion.

Memory snaps into place as understanding in present action. Her voice steadies.

MARA (CONT'D)
I did refuse the phantom rescue. I kept the lamp dark...

She looks at the dark lamp.

MARA (CONT'D)
...to keep the rock light for the real boat that passed silently.

MARA (CONT'D)
Not another keeper. Me. Writing as J. to make a choice I couldn't make as Mara.

CLOSE-UP: The phantom rescue entry re-read with new eyes. Boat lights in fog misread as a man calling. J. urging her to chase the lights, Mara refusing to abandon the watch.

MARA (CONT'D)
I misread the lights. I called it a man in the water. I refused to leave the lamp. The real boat passed on the mark I kept.

She closes the log page gently. Ink scratch AUDIO fades.

MARA (CONT'D)
I know what the lamp must be now.

CUT TO:

SC05
INT. LIGHTHOUSE BURNING ROOM - NIGHT

CONTINUOUS, same night.

VISUAL: Warm flare building, lamp beam sweeping across rocking sea.
AUDIO: Match strike, flare strengthening, radio static clearing, relief boat horn.

Mara stands at the lamp with wick trimmed. Strike. Flare catches, holds, blooms warm over salt crystals on the bench. Beam sweeps the sea for the first time.

Mara opens the log to the last page and writes, in her own steady hand, for the first time without a second hand.

SUPER - NEW ENTRY, in Mara's writing: Truth log. J. was me. Phantom was lights misread. Lamp kept dark in fear. Lamp lit now in duty. Relief boat in two days. - M. Quinn, on watch.

She signs it. No J.

MARA (CONT'D)
Mara Quinn. On watch. Lamp lit.

She keys the radio.

MARA (into radio)
Burning room to base. Lamp lit. Relief boat, burning room is guiding you.

RADIO VOICE (V.O.) (filtered by static)
Copy, burning room. We hear your horn -

From below the rock, a distant relief boat HORN answers. Clear. Radios do not make that sound. Real and close.

Mara looks out through the lit glass - not afraid the light will summon anything. The beam turns steadily over the black water.

VISUAL: Salt crystals glitter on the closed log in warm light. Lamp steady. Sea below the rock lit for the first time.

MARA (CONT'D) (quiet, to the sea)
Come home safe.

FADE OUT.
