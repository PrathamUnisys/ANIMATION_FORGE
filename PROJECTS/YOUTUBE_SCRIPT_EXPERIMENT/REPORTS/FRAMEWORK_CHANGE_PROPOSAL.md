# Proposed Animation Forge framework updates — experiment-informed

**Status:** Recommendation only. No governed framework file was modified.  
**Basis:** Editorial review of ten original scripts, ten revised scripts, twenty independent reviews plus targeted verification addenda, and public platform guidance. No audience data was collected.

## Current framework fit

The existing Audience Experience materials already cover much of what this experiment used:

- The draft Screenwriting Constitution asks for one promise across packaging, opening, progression, and ending; causal bridges; established rules; evidence-supported reveals; flexible scene purpose; and truthful runtime estimates.
- The Packaging Playbook requires an accurate title/thumbnail promise, comparison of multiple options, and post-click viewing context.
- The Retention Playbook says maps must follow the exact script, treats timing as a hypothesis, rejects artificial interruptions, and warns against reading analytics as causal proof.
- The Runtime Estimation Standard distinguishes `SCRIPT_ESTIMATE` from measured animatic/final durations and requires pace and hold assumptions.
- The Screenwriter and Script Critic instructions preserve revision lineage, independent review, evidence, and human approval boundaries.

These are already strong matches. The experiment does not justify adding a fixed hook interval, mandatory story structure, numeric retention target, or a pass/fail creative score.

## Evidence from this experiment

1. **Language accessibility is the clearest gap for this target format.** Several scripts had to define terms with ordinary examples. The user’s “simple language, mature ideas” rule worked as an editorial brief across all ten scripts, but the existing general Screenwriter instruction does not explicitly include a read-aloud or general-audience language check. This is an editorial observation, not audience validation.
2. **The existing promise and causality guidance caught the main packaging/story issues.** YS07’s unclear scenario, YS09’s weakly specific package, YS10’s literal “no cause” implication, and YS03’s sensational-looking image were resolved by applying existing principles; no new gate was needed.
3. **Deterministic metadata checks are useful and separate from creative judgement.** The final audit found a missing YS02 Retention Map row and stale metadata in YS09/YS10, even though the scripts’ creative content was otherwise coherent. A simple checklist can ask that counts, `SCRIPT_ESTIMATE`, headings, and map references match the exact revision. It should report mismatches, not judge story quality.
4. **Audience retention principles remain unproven.** No performance data supports turning any observed opening, pacing, analogy, or reveal pattern into a mandatory rule.

## Smallest useful recommendation

After the ten-script report is accepted, consider adding an **optional YouTube long-form narration check** to the relevant format guidance (likely a YouTube narration format page rather than global CORE or agent instructions):

- Read the narration aloud at the intended pace.
- Check that each sentence is understandable on first listen to the stated general audience; explain necessary terms in common words.
- Preserve mature subject matter and complexity while simplifying the language.
- Confirm the title/thumbnail promise appears in the opening and receives a fitting payoff.
- Mark runtime as `SCRIPT_ESTIMATE` until a timed recording or animatic exists.

This should be a format-specific editorial prompt, not a deterministic language score or an approval gate. Keep separate mechanical checks for word counts, runtime assumptions, and exact revision references where the chosen workflow supports them.

## Do not recommend from this sample

- No mandatory hook-by-second deadline, 30-second cliffhanger, fixed beat interval, or forced pattern interrupt.
- No global word-count target to reach a duration.
- No universal narrative structure or title formula.
- No “retention score,” arbitrary success threshold, or claim that the scripts predict virality.
- No direct CORE/agent/schema/runtime modification based on this editorial sample.
