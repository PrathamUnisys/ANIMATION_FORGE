# YS05 concept — The Bridge That Began to Twist

**Category:** Engineering failure / history
**Working title:** The Bridge That Began to Twist
**Alternates:** Why Tacoma Narrows Bridge Started to Twist; The Bridge That Wind Could Tear Apart; What Engineers Learned from Galloping Gertie
**Premise:** Tacoma Narrows Bridge opened in 1940 already moving in the wind. On November 7, its motion changed from familiar up-and-down waves to a twisting motion that grew until the center span failed. The story follows what engineers knew, what they missed, and what the collapse changed.
**Audience:** General viewers, 12+ accessible language; engineering and history curious.
**Central curiosity:** How could a bridge move from a gentle bounce to a destructive twist in a modest wind?
**Promised feeling:** Suspense that resolves into a clear physical explanation and a lesson about uncertainty in engineering.
**Click reason:** A familiar object begins moving in an impossible way.
**Continuation reason:** The motion changes form; failed fixes and a key change in the deck explain why the bridge became unstable.
**Distinctive angle:** Tell the event through the bridge’s changing motion, not as a simple “resonance did it” myth.
**Evidence required:** WSDOT history, contemporary investigation summaries, modern engineering analysis; distinguish the primary torsional-flutter explanation from unresolved details.
**Thumbnail:** Narrow bridge deck visibly twisting, with calm water below and a wind arrow. Text `WHY THE TWIST?`
**Credibility risks:** Oversimplifying the mechanism, claiming one single trigger is universally agreed, implying the wind was the only source of energy, or treating the famous film as complete evidence.
**Selection screen:** Strong escalating event, clear visual model, moderate technical risk manageable with simple diagrams. Selected for batch 3.
