# YS06 concept — How Shackleton’s Crew Survived the Endurance

**Category:** Survival / exploration history
**Working title:** How Shackleton’s Crew Survived the Endurance
**Alternates:** Their Ship Was Crushed in Antarctica. All 28 Survived.; The 800-Nautical-Mile Rescue from Elephant Island; When Endurance Was Crushed by Ice
**Premise:** When Antarctic ice trapped and destroyed Endurance, Shackleton’s expedition became a sequence of survival problems: drifting camp, open boats, an exposed island, and a dangerous voyage for help.
**Audience:** General viewers, 12+ accessible language; adventure, history, and survival audiences.
**Central curiosity:** Once the ship was gone and no one knew where the crew was, what practical choices gave them a chance to get home?
**Promised feeling:** Anxiety turning into respect for planning, skill, cooperation, and luck.
**Click reason:** A ship is lost in Antarctic ice, but the crew faces a much longer escape.
**Continuation reason:** Every refuge is temporary; each solution creates the next problem.
**Distinctive angle:** Keep the focus on successive choices and constraints, not a simplistic “great leader alone saves everyone” story.
**Evidence required:** Scott Polar Research Institute history, expedition accounts/diaries, dates and distances checked carefully; avoid invented dialogue or inner thoughts.
**Thumbnail:** Three small boats beneath a vast wall of ice; one route line toward a tiny island. Text `NO WAY HOME?`
**Credibility risks:** Simplifying crew count, mixing nautical and statute miles, dramatizing weather, omitting the separate Ross Sea party when discussing the whole expedition, romanticizing decisions, or inventing private thoughts.
**Selection screen:** Strong event progression and low-cost map/ice/boat visuals; factual density and scope controlled by focusing on Endurance’s 28-person Weddell Sea crew. Selected for batch 3.
