## Narrative, clarity, and production

The sequence from initial bouncing (01:05), to the morning timeline (02:30), to the torsional-flutter explanation (04:30), and limits of the explanation (06:30) is logical. The explanation “the bridge’s own movement helped change how the wind pushed it” is accessible, and the swing analogy is clearly an analogy rather than a literal model. The script distinguishes a well-supported main explanation from unresolved details.

The visual directions are feasible: a simple bridge drawing, wind-tunnel model, clock, and airflow arrows can explain the mechanism without demanding elaborate simulation. The dog and driver episode adds human stakes; the note to avoid a distressing close-up is appropriate.

## Evidence and credibility
