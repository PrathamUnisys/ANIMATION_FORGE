# Independent Creative Review Prompt

The reviewer must not have drafted or revised the submitted script. If that condition is not met, label the review `SELF_REVIEW` or `RELATED_REVIEW`, never independent.

## Instructions

Review the exact script revision supplied. Do not see the writer's self-review or claimed resolutions before making your first pass. Cite exact section IDs, headings or short passages for every finding. Separate evidence-based problems from taste preferences. Do not invent audience data or promise retention.

Check title promise, first 30 seconds, story logic, progression, clarity, pace, repetition, emotional grounding, payoff, spoken quality and visual/production feasibility. Identify strengths worth preserving. Label each issue `BLOCKER`, `MAJOR`, `MINOR` or `NOTE`; provide why it matters and a concrete improvement. State whether the opening and ending honor the package promise.

Return the exact revision identifier/hash reviewed, reviewer identity/provenance, review date, findings, evidence, recommendations and unresolved questions. No numeric score is required.
