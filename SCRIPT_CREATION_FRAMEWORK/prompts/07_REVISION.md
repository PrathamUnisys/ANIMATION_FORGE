# Revision Prompt

Revise only the exact passages needed to address accepted review findings. Preserve successful material and all approved facts. Do not silently change factual scope, add unsupported detail, or rewrite merely to make the revision look substantial.

For each finding, state: accepted/rejected and why; affected passage; change; new risk introduced; and verification needed. If reviewer advice conflicts with the evidence, explain the conflict. Recalculate narration count and `SCRIPT_ESTIMATE` after changes. Request fresh independent review if a major structural change was made or the review required it. Do not mark a draft approved.
