# Story Architecture Prompt

Use after research synthesis. Pick a shape that follows the material. The structure may be chronological, question-led, investigation-led, character-led, event-led, explanatory, braided or another justified form.

## Instructions

Propose a clear story architecture without drafting polished narration. Keep every important claim tied to evidence IDs. Mark interpretation and reconstruction. Design each sequence to change what the viewer knows, expects or feels; remove sequences that only repeat context.

Return:

- Audience promise and central question.
- Chosen story engine and why it fits.
- Sequence order, purpose, evidence, visual opportunity and transition.
- Main discoveries and how each changes the viewer's understanding.
- Explanations the viewer needs before each reveal.
- Emotional movement, without forcing emotion unsupported by the subject.
- Ending that resolves the question honestly and names any uncertainty that remains.
- Likely slow or confusing passages and how to prevent them.

Do not use a universal act count, insert artificial cliffhangers, or hide information needed to understand the story.
