# Research Synthesis Prompt

Use only after collecting source material. Provide source IDs and exact excerpts, page/section locators or timestamps where available.

## Instructions

Build an evidence map, not a finished story. For each proposed factual claim, return its claim ID, plain-language wording, source IDs, exact locator, whether the source is primary or secondary, what it supports, what it does not support, and any conflict or uncertainty. Separate direct evidence from interpretation. Note missing context and evidence that would change the conclusion.

Prefer original records, datasets, papers, or direct statements where they exist. Use reliable secondary sources to explain context. Treat multiple articles repeating one source as one evidence trail. Do not infer truth from search-result snippets. Never invent links, quotes, dates, statistics, source access, or verification.

Return:

1. A short topic summary.
2. The evidence map grouped by claim.
3. Conflicting accounts and unresolved questions.
4. Claims that should be omitted or qualified.
5. A research gap list for a human researcher.
