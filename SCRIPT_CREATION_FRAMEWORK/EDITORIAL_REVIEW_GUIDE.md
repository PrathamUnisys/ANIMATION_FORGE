# Editorial Review Guide

**Status:** Proposed. The module has no live review orchestration yet.

## Independence

The critic reads the exact title/thumbnail package, full script and stated audience/runtime. On first review, do not provide writer self-assessment, claimed resolutions or revision rationale. Record whether the reviewer is a separate agent/person, a distinct model invocation, or the author conducting self-review. Self-review must never be labelled independent. A separate fact-check is also needed; creative review alone is not fact clearance.

## Review order

1. Read the script without the Retention Map and self-assessment.
2. Note central question, promise, structure and ending in the critic's own words.
3. Assess title/thumbnail fit, opening, comprehension, progression, factual framing, production feasibility and payoff as relevant.
4. Read the map and companion metadata; report exact-revision mismatches.
5. Check factual claims against cited sources only if this review was explicitly assigned and access is available; otherwise mark them unchecked.

## Finding format

For each finding record severity (`BLOCKER`, `MAJOR`, `MINOR`, `NOTE`), exact scene/timecode/paragraph and quoted or unmistakable passage, observable evidence, interpretation, consequence, recommendation, confidence and limits. Distinguish fact, interpretation and taste. Avoid generic “make it more engaging.”

## Dimensions

- Packaging promise and title scope.
- First-listen orientation and opening delivery.
- Central narrative question and story direction.
- New information, causality and progression.
- Exposition density, repetition and transitions.
- Plain spoken language without loss of subject depth.
- Evidence scope, uncertainty and attribution.
- Visual/audio purpose and production feasibility.
- Emotional credibility and ethical handling.
- Ending and opening-promise delivery.
- Runtime evidence and exact-revision metadata.

## Severity

- **BLOCKER:** an integrity, prerequisite, central logic or critical evidence defect makes responsible use/review impossible.
- **MAJOR:** substantial comprehension, causal, factual framing, narrative or production problem.
- **MINOR:** localized weakness with limited impact.
- **NOTE:** optional craft possibility, preference or uncertainty.

Do not use numerical scores or average gates. A critic cannot rewrite, approve or change canon unless separately assigned under a human-authorized workflow.

## Revision response

For each major/blocker, record `ADDRESSED`, `PARTIALLY_ADDRESSED`, `NOT_CHANGED` or `DEFERRED`, with before/after passage references and reason. Re-read changed passages and downstream consequences. New revision, word count, runtime estimate, map and checks are linked to exact source hash. A material change after the second review should trigger another review or an explicit “not re-reviewed” note.
